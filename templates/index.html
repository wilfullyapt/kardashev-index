Why joules

Progress has a unit.

Every civilization that ever mattered was a machine for turning energy into possibility. We think the builders doing that work should be measured — and seen.

  1. I

    Energy is the substrate.

    Every model trained, every factory run, every launch is energy put to work. A civilization’s reach is its power budget, and the companies that grow it widen what everyone can do.

  2. II

    Measure, don’t emote.

    Each score carries a written justification and links to its sources. Disagree with the evidence, not the mood. Vibes are not data.

  3. III

    The gradient points up.

    Compute, energy, open markets, permission to build, a culture that ships — five forces that move us toward a planet’s worth of power, then a star’s. The light is worth climbing toward.

The Kardashev Ladder

Each rung is ten billion times the last.

Proposed by Nikolai Kardashev in 1964; interpolated by Carl Sagan as K = (log₁₀P − 6) / 10, with P in watts.
